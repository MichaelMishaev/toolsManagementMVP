# Lift Pro 26 commercial site

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Israeli warehouse owners, small and medium contractors, infrastructure companies, quarries, ports, and logistics operations evaluating equipment for real operating needs.

## Product Purpose

Present Lift Pro 26 Israel's verified equipment range, direct-sourcing model, local service and spare-parts value, and manufacturer documents so a visitor can identify a relevant equipment family and inspect a model without starting from raw PDF files.

## Positioning

Lift Pro 26 combines direct international sourcing and a lean operating structure with a local Israeli service address, manufacturer-backed model data, and spare-parts sourcing.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate excavators, mini excavators, wheel loaders, electric equipment, service support, and original manufacturer specifications before making contact or placing an order outside this site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Hebrew-first RTL commercial website.
- Semantic HTML catalogue with 22 public models, one standalone URL per model, quick previews, related-model navigation, and optional source-PDF downloads.
- No invented prices, stock, delivery times, finance terms, warranty terms, certifications, testimonials, or contact details.
- No form or message is sent because an approved commercial contact destination is not yet recorded.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand: Lift Pro 26 Israel / ליפט פרו 26 ישראל בע״מ.
- Canonical logo: `../assets/brand/lift-pro-26-logo-transparent.png`, preserved uncropped on its transparent canvas.
- Core line: `ציוד גדול. חשיבה חדשה. מחיר שעובד בשביל העסק שלך.`
- User-pinned direction: Apple-inspired 2026 product design principles, without copying Apple branding or exact layouts.

## Evidence on Hand

- Company/about facts: `data/site-content.json`.
- Brand policy and approved artwork: `../assets/brand/README.md` and `../assets/brand/lift-pro-26-logo-transparent.png`.
- Model specifications and imagery: approved PDFs under `catalogue/` and derived product images under `assets/catalogue/`.
- Hero photography: the LOVOL FL918 field image under `assets/hero/` is derived directly from the approved manufacturer loader catalogue and keeps the machine, lighting, and work environment in one verified photograph.

## Product Principles

- Start with the visitor's job and equipment family, then reveal image-led model stories and full model detail pages.
- Keep manufacturer source evidence one step away from every technical claim.
- Make service and ownership value clear without overpromising.
- Preserve native scrolling, predictable navigation, and direct user control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Hebrew-first RTL, responsive from narrow mobile upward, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency support.
